CausalTAD: Injecting Causal Knowledge into Large Language Models for Tabular Anomaly Detection

Ruiqi Wang, Ruikang Liu, Runyu Chen, Haoxiang Suo, Zhiyi Peng, Zhuo Tang, Changjian Chen

A causal-aware framework that reorders and reweights table columns to preserve inter-column dependencies when large language models process tabular data.

Paper Code
Abstract

Large language models often serialize table columns without considering their causal structure. CausalTAD learns inter-column relationships, uses them to order and reweight columns, and improves anomaly detection across more than 30 benchmark datasets.
